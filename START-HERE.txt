RESEARCH REPORT READER 4.8 — WEBSITE

UPDATE YOUR EXISTING SITE
1. Open Workers & Pages in Cloudflare and select your existing reader project.
2. Choose Create a new deployment, select Production, and upload WEBSITE-UPLOAD.zip.
3. Select Deploy, then open your usual .pages.dev website link in a fresh tab.
   If GitHub provided a verified-website.zip wrapper, extract it once first.
   The WEBSITE-UPLOAD.zip itself should be uploaded as-is.

FIRST DEPLOYMENT ONLY
In Workers & Pages, choose Create application. If the Workers page offers
“Continue to Pages”, use that link. Choose the Pages direct-upload option,
create your project, upload WEBSITE-UPLOAD.zip and select Deploy site.
Readers just need the public site link. No app store, npm, or account needed.

HOW TO READ ON A PHONE
1. Save the research PDF on your device. Open the reader link and tap Choose
   PDF files; look in Files or Downloads. One Argus file is enough to start.
   Add another source attaches other Argus reports for that company later.
2. Tap View financials & forecasts (or View forecasts & revisions), then
   Read one metric. All reported years for that metric are listed vertically.
3. Tap a number with a PDF page to see the original. Back or Close takes you
   back to the figures. The full-width tables and all years remain available.

Share reader link sends the website address, not your reports. Add to Home
Screen is optional; it puts an icon on your phone.

SESSION BEHAVIOR
Reports stay in memory in the current tab. You can switch between them while the
tab remains open. Refreshing, closing, or leaving the page clears the session.
Clear session removes everything immediately after confirmation. Original files
on the device are unchanged. Keep the downloaded PDFs to open them again later.
Mobile browsers may discard a background tab, which also ends the session.
There are no accounts, cloud uploads, report storage, or cross-device syncing.

The website ZIP is ready for upload; building it does not publish it.
